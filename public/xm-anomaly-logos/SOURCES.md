# XM Anomaly logo sources

Images in this directory are locally optimized copies of historical XM Anomaly badge or season artwork catalogued by [Ingress Plus](https://ingress.plus/badges). They remain property of their respective rights holders and are used by this non-commercial fan archive for event identification.

`Cassandra` and `13MAGNUS` predate the anomaly medal series. The interface deliberately falls back to the period-appropriate generic Ingress mark rather than assigning a later event medal to them.
